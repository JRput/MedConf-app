Location

Venue

ICM-International Congress Centre of the Munich Messe

The European Lung Cancer Congress (ELCC 2027), jointly organised by ESMO and IASLC in collaboration with partner societies representing the multidisciplinary thoracic oncology community, will take place from 17 to 20 March 2027 in Munich, Germany, with sessions also available through a dedicated virtual platform.

It brings together medical oncologists, radiation oncologists, thoracic surgeons, respiratory physicians/pneumologists, interventional radiologists and pathologists to advance science, share education and enhance daily practice for professionals caring for people with lung and other thoracic cancers.

The scientific and educational programme explores the latest developments in thoracic oncology, including targeted therapies, immunotherapy and novel treatment strategies, emerging diagnostic tools, biomarkers and artificial intelligence, as well as multidisciplinary approaches to complex cases, patient care and supportive care.

Be part of this highly interactive meeting, featuring a practice-shaping programme, cross-specialty dialogue and actionable take-home insights. Connect with peers, debate emerging evidence and contribute your expertise to help move thoracic oncology forward.
