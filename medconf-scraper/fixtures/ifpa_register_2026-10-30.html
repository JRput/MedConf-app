Registration fees (no VAT applies)

1) EUR values are indicative only. Fees are billed in SEK. 

Registration categories


The registration fee includes access to the main scientific sessions and coffee breaks. Additional items may be added to the included program.  
 

Health Care Professionals (Prescribers) – those who are involved in the provision of healthcare to patients, such as physicians, nurses, pharmacists, and other licensed clinicians and are authorized in their own country to prescribe or supply prescription‑only medicines (POM). They will receive full access to all areas of the Conference, including the industry exhibition and satellite symposia.

Health Care Professionals (Non-Prescribers) – those who are involved in the provision of healthcare to patients, such as physicians, nurses, pharmacists, and other licensed clinicians but not authorized in their own country to prescribe or supply prescription‑only medicines (POM). They will have access to all Conference areas except the industry exhibition and satellite symposia.

Students – are medical doctors enrolled in a training program leading to specialization. The applicant must be 40 years of age or younger. Proof of status is required during registration. 

Non-Clinical Attendees – those who don’t fit into other categories. 

  • LMIC countries: Delegates attending from low-and-middle-income countries (see list) – may request a discount by contacting us via registration@ifpa-pso.com
  • Paying IFPA members: Paying IFPA members may request a discount by contacting us via registration@ifpa-pso.com. 

Registration policies 

Cancellation and refunds – All cancellation requests must be submitted in writing to: registration@ifpa-pso.com. The cancellation deadline is 23:59 CET on 19 February, 2027. An administrative fee of SEK 550 will apply.

Participant and name changes – Name changes can be made through your personal booking page until 23:59 CET on 19 February, 2027. After this deadline, changes may still be requested until 6 May, 2027, subject to an administrative fee of SEK 350.
