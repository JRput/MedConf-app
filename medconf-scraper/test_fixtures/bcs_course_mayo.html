BCS & Mayo Clinic Cardiology Review Course 2027

The BCS and Mayo Clinic Cardiology Review Course 2027 is the premier opportunity for consultants and cardiology residents to update their knowledge in cardiology and prepare for revalidation. Held over 5 days, this comprehensive course presented by leading figures from the UK and Mayo Clinic covers the European Cardiology Curriculum through a broad range of didactic lectures together with case-based interactive sessions. Please note that this course is in person only and not hybrid.


Venue Address: Royal College of Physicians, London
