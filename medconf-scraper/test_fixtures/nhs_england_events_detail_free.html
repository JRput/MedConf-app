Progress Review, Shared Learning & Planning Ahead:

Join us for the in-person meeting for Viral Hepatitis Primary Care and GP Champion Network, this follows the productive session we had in early May. This October meeting will provide an opportunity to reconnect, review progress made nationally and continue strengthening our collaborative approach towards Viral Hepatitis Elimination.

Bringing together GP Champions, ODN representatives, and key stakeholders, the session will focus on sharing updates, reflecting on achievements and challenges, and identifying priorities and opportunities for the months ahead.

We will explore examples of successful initiatives, discuss lessons learned, and consider how effective approaches can be adapted and implemented across local teams to further improve Viral hepatitis pathways.

The meeting will also provide valuable time for networking, open discussion, and collaborative problem-solving as we maintain momentum towards our shared elimination goals.

We look forward to your continued involvement and contribution to the ongoing success of the elimination programme.
