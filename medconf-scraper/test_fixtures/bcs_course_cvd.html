CVD Prevention Course

19 April 2027

Location: Royal College of Physicians, London

Overview and Key reasons to attend

Cardiovascular disease prevention is changing rapidly. The tools have never been better. New lipid-lowering, cardiometabolic and anti-inflammatory therapies are changing what can be achieved. Healthcare professionals across different specialities have an important role to play in using these therapies and leading the pathways that bring them into clinical practice.

This one-day course showcases the cutting edge in CVD prevention and makes the case for prevention as a core cardiology responsibility. A national and international expert faculty will cover:

  • Secondary prevention and lipids, including LDL-C targets, emerging therapies, lipoprotein(a) and familial hypercholesterolaemia
  • The cardio-renal-metabolic interface: obesity, diabetes, CKD, hypertension and lifestyle
  • Interventional prevention, CT coronary imaging, antiplatelet strategy and inflammation
  • How to build and lead cardiology-run prevention pathways, from tertiary risk clinics to community outreach
  • The day will close with a patient interview and interactive simulated prevention MDT
  • Delegates will leave ready to make prevention part of the everyday cardiology practice

Who should attend

  • Consultant cardiologists
  • Cardiology trainees
  • Clinical pharmacists, cardiac specialist nurses and advanced care practitioners working within or running prevention services
  • Lipidologists and physicians in related specialties: diabetes and endocrinology and general and acute medicine
  • GPs with an extended role in cardiology or a special interest
