When NHS England launched its Commitment to Carers  programme in May 2014, it made a commitment to host an annual conference bringing together unpaid carers and organisations working to improve support for carers across England.

We are delighted to invite you to join us for the 10th Commitment to Carers Annual Conference, which will take place on Tuesday 1 December, 10:30am–4:00pm, online via Microsoft Teams.

This milestone event provides an opportunity for unpaid carers, Integrated Care Boards (ICBs), Integrated Care Systems (ICSs), regional carers leads, commissioners, local authority partners, voluntary sector organisations and healthcare providers to come together to share learning, celebrate progress and explore how we can continue to improve the identification, recognition and support of unpaid carers.

As we celebrate10 years of the Commitment to Carers Annual Conference, this year's theme is:"Ambitions into Action – Together"

Through keynote speakers, lived experience, workshops and examples of innovative practice, the conference will explore how we can work collaboratively to turn our shared ambitions into meaningful improvements for unpaid carers and the people they care for.

Please note: Places are limited and will be allocated on a first-come, first-served basis. You will receive further information on how to choose a workshop place at a later date. 

Registration opens on Friday 1st October at 5pm and closes on Friday 13th November at 5pm.

Please do share with your colleagues and networks and we look forward to welcoming you to the conference.
