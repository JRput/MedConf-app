## Synthesis (foreman) — answer to the owner's question
**No. Today this is not the case for all sources, and a new source does not get it automatically.** The shared discovery lives only in the nightly remediator; at scrape time it is hand-coded per extractor; venue discovery does not exist outside one family; a row with one tier is never improved; currency recognition is split across five parsers that disagree, two of which read only £ $ €, and unknown currencies silently become GBP; nothing in CI goes red when any of this fails. The empirical sample says the main page is NOT where fees are missed — they are on ticketing hosts (Eventbrite), script-rendered booking apps, external organiser sites, and in European number formats, or they are not published at all. Abstract "gaps" are mostly CPD events with no call; venue gaps are page banners and external sites.
